---
name: Raw-material sourcing
description: User-approved rules for Hillen99 prices, units, publishing and safe catalog updates.
---

Import Hillen99 raw-material listings at their original supplier price; ingredients listed per gram stay per gram, while whole sets are priced per item. The user chose to publish imported materials and retain native supplier currency rather than add a markup.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved the original supplier prices and publication. Some supplier listings are sets with a per-item price, not per-gram ingredients.

**How to apply:** Keep source-price snapshots separate from Maya's editable price and currency. On a repeat import, update supplier metadata without overwriting a merchant's name, description, category, price, currency, image or publish choice. Supplier availability describes Hillen99 only; do not present it as Maya's stock.
